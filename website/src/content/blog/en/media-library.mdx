Thunder’s media library brings local audio and video together without moving the original files.

## Add content

Add individual files or a folder. Thunder creates library records and keeps links to their original locations.

## Persistent playlists

Playlists stay between launches. Move records between them, change display names, and remove entries without physically deleting files.

## Up Next

Up Next is a transient queue with priority over the active playlist. Use it to change what plays next without editing the saved list.

The transient queue clears after restart, while playlists and the library remain.
