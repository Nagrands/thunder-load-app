The right format depends on where you plan to use the file.

## Ready-to-play video

Choose regular video when the file should open immediately in most players. Thunder can merge video and audio streams with FFmpeg when needed.

## Source audio

Audio without MP3 preserves the source format. It is a good choice for later editing or when you want to avoid an extra conversion.

## MP3

MP3 is useful for broad compatibility with devices and music apps. Conversion takes additional time and cannot improve the source quality.

## A specific quality

Resolution is not the only signal. Check codec, FPS, bitrate, and whether audio is included. If you are unsure, start with Auto or Best.
