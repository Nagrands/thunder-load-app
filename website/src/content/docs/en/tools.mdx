## Workspace

Tools are always available and do not require a separate module switch in Settings.

## Included tools

- **Hash Check** calculates checksums and compares files.
- **Media Inspector** displays containers, streams, codecs, bitrate, HDR, and subtitles through `ffprobe`.
- **Backup** uses profiles, filters, and system archiving tools.
- **WG Unlock** runs platform-specific WireGuard recovery scenarios.
- **Quick shortcuts** open system utilities and are primarily designed for Windows.
